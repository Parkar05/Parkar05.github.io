B2B Telecom Churn Prediction

A LightGBM model that flags B2B telecom customers likely to churn, and uses SHAP to explain each prediction. Account managers can see who is at risk and why. It's served as a Streamlit app with a Power BI view.

The problem

Losing a B2B account takes out many lines and a lot of recurring revenue at once. The commercial team wanted a list of at-risk customers early enough to step in.

What I built

  • Data preparation and feature engineering on customer, usage and billing attributes
  • LightGBM model training, tuning and evaluation
  • SHAP explanations: global drivers and per-customer reasons
  • Risk scoring served through a Streamlit app
  • Power BI view of risk scores for the commercial team
  • Synthetic Kuwait B2B data (KWD); code and docs on GitHub
